Abstract

Enterprise teams split operational truth across CSV cohort tables, BI KPI dashboards, recorded escalation calls, and engineering RFCs. Standard flat-text RAG pipelines strip spreadsheet schemas, lose speaker timestamps, and risk leaking confidential or cross-region spans when access control runs as an afterthought.

QuickSpaces implements the platform layer of an agentic enterprise workspace modeled on Amazon Quick Core Experience, Quick Spaces, and AWS Quick RAG: modality-aware ingestion (scanning PII columns on write), strict tenant/region/clearance ACL pre-filtering prior to hybrid BM25 + vector scoring, cross-modal synthesis via Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) with auto-generated BI charts, and an autonomous Governance Onboarding Agent that audits and remediates account misconfigurations in one click.

02Problem Statement & Subsystem Design

An enterprise digital workspace has to solve four coupled systems problems simultaneously: unifying writes from heterogeneous producer pipelines (Snowflake CSV syncs, QuickSight metric publishers, Chime call transcripts, Git RFCs), preserving modality-native structure during chunking, guaranteeing zero cross-clearance leakage during retrieval, and continuously auditing account-level governance drift.

Subsystem Amazon Quick Surface Engineering Mechanism Implemented
Spaces & Asset Metadata Quick Core Experience & Quick Spaces Multi-tenant Space catalog indexing files, favorites, producer lineage, clearance tiers (PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL), and regional residency tags.
Multi-Modal Ingestion AWS Quick RAG Pipeline Typed CSV parser (computing column rollups + regex/header PII column detection), [MM:SS] speaker-turn VTT parser, BI KPI series extractor, and heading-anchored RFC splitter.
Zero-Leakage Hybrid Search AWS Quick Information Retrieval Pre-filters assets against caller clearance & region prior to Okapi BM25 + 4-gram cosine Reciprocal Rank Fusion (k=20) and asset-level max-pooling.
Governance Onboarding Agent Enterprise Account Governance Evaluates 5 controls (GOV-PII-01..GOV-RET-05), enforces dynamic PII masking for non-Admin viewers, and emits SHA-256 hash-chained remediation receipts.
